Most Copilot rollouts we audit have the same root cause for trouble: governance was an afterthought. Here is the 30-day plan we run with clients before turning Copilot loose.

## Week 1 — Readiness

- Inventory sensitivity labels and DLP policies (or build the baseline if none exist).
- Run a data oversharing assessment with **Microsoft Purview**.
- Define the pilot cohort, success metrics, and an explicit exit criterion.

## Week 2 — Guardrails

- Apply sensitivity labels to your top 50 SharePoint sites.
- Enable **Restricted SharePoint Search** while you remediate oversharing.
- Configure DLP for Copilot prompts and responses.

## Week 3 — Pilot

- Deliver three Copilot scenarios per persona (Sales, Finance, Engineering are great starts).
- Capture qualitative and quantitative metrics weekly.
- Hold a daily 15-minute office hour — adoption is built by support, not training videos.

## Week 4 — Scale or stop

- Compare pilot metrics to the success criteria you set in Week 1.
- Either expand to the next cohort, or pause and address the root cause.
- Decide what to productize: which scenarios became Copilot Studio agents?

## The TL;DR

Copilot adoption is 20% licenses and 80% governance + change management. Plan it that way.
